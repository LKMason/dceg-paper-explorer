DCEG / PAPER EXPLORER

A landscape of research

Each point is one paper’s title and available abstract. Drag to rotate the map, or switch projections to see a different view of the same collection.

Search: highlight a query and browse its ranked results in the list panel. Select a paper to read its details.

Neighbors: select paper A to explore its nearest papers. Choose a neighbor to read A and B together; “Make A” moves your starting point to B. The search bar can find a starting paper. Neighborhood colors override all other coloring: A is black, the ten nearest papers are blue, B is coral, and other papers are faded.

Compare: search for two ideas, authors, groups, or branches. Semantic queries contribute their top 50 papers; author, group, and branch suggestions contribute all exact matches. Blue marks A only, coral marks B only, and black marks papers in both sets. Filter the list by any of those sets. Switching modes preserves each mode’s work.

Add paper: enter any PubMed ID. An existing paper is highlighted; a new record is fetched from PubMed and embedded locally using its title and available abstract. Additions join every mode and are saved in this browser. PCA uses the fitted transform; new UMAP and t-SNE positions are estimated from nearby base papers. Neighbor distances use the full embeddings. New-paper embeddings use the quantized browser model, so they may differ slightly from the base papers’ full-precision embeddings.

Paper buddy: each author is a uniform distribution over all their papers in this collection. Candidates are ranked by debiased Sinkhorn divergence using the original 768-dimensional embeddings, with cosine ground cost (half the squared Euclidean distance for normalized vectors). Lower is closer. Every paper gets equal weight within its author’s portfolio. The score is OTε(A,B) − ½OTε(A,A) − ½OTε(B,B), including the entropic penalty in each OT term. Smoothing ε defaults to 0.05; the candidate minimum defaults to three papers. Shared papers are retained; optionally exclude all existing coauthors. Blue shows the source author, coral the selected buddy, and black their shared papers. Names are used as published, without identity disambiguation; profiles cover this collection, not an author’s entire publication history. Sparse portfolios give limited evidence. Method: Feydy et al. (2019).

Neighbor distances use the original 768-dimensional vectors, independently of the 3D projection. Papers can have multiple branch annotations; missing branch information is shown as unlisted.

Search stays in your browser

The demo topics have real, precomputed query embeddings and work offline. New queries download a quantized EmbeddingGemma 2 text model (about 230 MB including its tokenizer and runtime) on first use. Inference and ranking then run locally; queries and paper text are never uploaded.

Use a recent Chrome or Edge for WebGPU acceleration. A slower WebAssembly fallback is available. Quantized browser queries can produce slightly different rankings from the full-precision precomputed demo queries.
